## 0.3.0 — release candidate

- Keep page content and symbol mappings consistent within a diagram session.

This entry describes implemented changes. Release approval requires the evidence
in `docs/VALIDATION.md` and the checklist in `docs/PUBLISHING.md`; an unchecked or
unavailable test remains an open release gate.
